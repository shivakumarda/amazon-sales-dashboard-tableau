# Amazon Sales Dashboard (Sales Performance Analysis 2025)

An interactive Tableau dashboard that analyzes e-commerce sales performance across time, geography, product categories, payment modes and customers.

> Note: This is a portfolio project built on a sample dataset. It is not affiliated with Amazon.

## Business Problem
Which categories, regions and customers drive sales and profit, and how did performance change over the year? The dashboard helps a business track revenue, profitability and customer behavior at a glance.

## Tools Used
- Tableau (dashboard design, maps, calculated fields, KPI cards)
- Data cleaning and preparation

## Key Metrics (KPIs)
| Metric | Value |
|---|---|
| Total Sales | 84.71 Cr |
| Total Profit | 20.32 Cr |
| Avg Order Value | 84,711.33 |
| Avg Discount % | 15.15% |
| Total Orders | 10,000 |
| Total Quantity Sold | 24,917 |

## Dashboard Views
- **Monthly Sales and Profit Trend:** month-wise movement across 2025
- **Sales by State:** geographic map of sales across India
- **Profit vs Sales:** category-level comparison of profit against sales
- **Sales by Category:** Electronics, Home & Kitchen, Fashion, Sports, Beauty, Books
- **Payment Mode:** Net Banking, COD, UPI, Debit Card, Credit Card, Amazon Pay
- **Top 10 Customers:** highest-spending customers

## Key Insights
- Electronics is the largest category at 25.36 Cr, about 30% of total sales, followed by Home & Kitchen (17.58 Cr) and Fashion (17.36 Cr).
- Overall profit margin is about 24% (20.32 Cr profit on 84.71 Cr sales).
- Monthly sales peaked in May (9.04 Cr), then dropped sharply from June to July and stayed lower for the rest of the year. Profit followed the same pattern.
- Payment modes are fairly evenly split, with Net Banking and COD slightly ahead.
